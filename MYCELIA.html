Why MYCELIA?

A research map you can see and replay, instead of a stack of tabs you have to remember.

Tab-heavy research

MYCELIA

Question
Tabs, PDFs and notes apps
MYCELIA
Where things live
Papers, data, notes and results sit in separate tabs and apps.
One canvas holds papers, datasets, code, notes and experiments as linked cards.
How sources connect
You remember which paper relates to which.
Links are drawn from shared topics, with the reason shown on the edge.
Getting help
A separate chat for each question, with no shared context.
Three specialist agents and a Controller work on the same map.
Staying in control
You copy and paste results across yourself.
Every agent action waits for Approve or Reject, and agents earn or lose score.
Trying an idea
Re-run a notebook to see what changes.
A what-if drawer shows predicted accuracy, latency and memory as you drag a slider (simulated).
Remembering how you got here
Browser history and scattered file versions.
A replayable timeline of every source, link and experiment.
Working offline
Depends on each app.
Everything is cached on your device and syncs when you reconnect.
Sharing
Screenshots and pasted links.
One Markdown or JSON export with notes, relationships and experiments.

Where tabs still win. Reading one paper start to finish is better in a PDF viewer. MYCELIA keeps the summary, the tags and the links; you keep the PDF. Experiments here are simulated, so treat the numbers as a guide for what to try next, not as results.
